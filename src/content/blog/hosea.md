The book of Hosea begins The Book of the Twelve, commonly referred to as The Minor Prophets, found in the Old Testament. The message that God speaks through His prophet Hosea is one that provokes not the return to a particularly line of thinking, but the return of heart to Him. Hosea must be read with the heart, for therein, the LORD is speaking to His people heart-to-heart.

The primary theme of the book of Hosea is God's call for His people to return to _Him_. They have forsaken Him and chosen for themselves other gods. The LORD expresses this reality vividly throughout His word through Hosea using marriage as an illustration, with Israel being pictured as an adulteress wife of harlotry. The LORD instructs Hosea to make this reality visible, and to fellowship with Him, by taking unto himself a wife of harlotry.
