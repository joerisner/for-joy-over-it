## Intro

When seeking to initiate your children and shepherd them into a place of maturity, many of us start by answering a question similar to "Which virtues would I like to see valued and exhibited by my children both now, and in their adulthood?"

## Questions to answer

_See 12/28/23 journal entry_

1. If I teach my kids that having certain virtues is important, does that nullify their need to trust Jesus for their maturity, sanctification, righteousness, justification, etc.
2. Is morality (goodness), in and of itself, valued by God?
3. Do we start (when our children are young) by teaching virtues and later inform them that their hope is in Christ alone?
4. The Bible talks much about "uprightness" and similar qualities. Is that the same as morality?
5. Is there are a difference in approach between the NT and OT in relation to teaching these things?
6. Is it biblical to have specific family values that are emphasized above others and actively try to teach adherence to those values?
7. Am I trying to apply the doctrine of justification by faith in Christ alone to learning virtues?
